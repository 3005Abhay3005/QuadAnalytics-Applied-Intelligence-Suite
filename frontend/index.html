Clinical Decision Support

Our diabetes and oncology models are trained on peer-reviewed population datasets. They surface statistically-driven risk assessments with full explainability — so practitioners understand exactly which biomarkers drive each prediction.

Automotive Asset Valuation

The car price estimator combines gradient-boosted regression with a rich feature schema — fuel type, brand tier, age, mileage, and transmission — to deliver residual-value estimates grounded in real market transaction data from Quikr.

Live Sports Intelligence

The IPL Score Forecaster applies regression over historical match telemetry to estimate first-innings totals in real time. Inputs include batting team, bowling team, current run rate, wickets fallen, and overs bowled.

Methodology

From raw data to explainable prediction.

1

Data Ingestion

Curated datasets from Kaggle, UCI, and academic sources. Validated for class balance, missing values, and outlier distribution.

2

Preprocessing

StandardScaler normalization, categorical encoding, and stratified train/test splits applied consistently across all models.

3

Model Training

scikit-learn estimators trained with cross-validation. Hyperparameters tuned via GridSearchCV on validation folds.

4

Evaluation

Models evaluated on held-out test sets. Confusion matrices, ROC-AUC, F1, and regression metrics recorded per model.

5

Deployment

Serialized with joblib, served via FastAPI. Real-time inference with feature attribution for every prediction.
